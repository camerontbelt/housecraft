# Tutorial 15, Cabinet Layout: following it in HouseCraft

Keep this open beside **Chief Architect Tutorial Guide › Kitchen and Bath Tutorials › Cabinet Layout**
(pages 260–276). It carries on from [Tutorial 14](14-cabinet-styles.md). The steps also run as the automatic test
[15-cabinet-layout.json](15-cabinet-layout.json).

## Moving cabinets (pages 261–262)

*Not in HouseCraft yet:* bumping and pushing (a cabinet stopping at a wall, then pushing its neighbour along). Cabinets
snap to walls and to each other as you drag; drag each one into place.

## Placing and resizing (pages 262–265)

1. **Base Cabinet**: one more along the left wall, one in the middle of the room, one in the bottom-left corner,
   and one along the Dining room wall beside it.
2. **Corner cabinet:** *not in HouseCraft yet:* automatic diagonal corner cabinets. Open the corner cabinet and set
   **Shape** to **Angled**, cutting one front corner back 12".
3. Widths: drag the side handles, or open the cabinet and type `30"`, `36"`, `18"`. The label (B30, B36…) follows.
4. **Center Object** (edit toolbar), then click the middle window: the sink base centres on it.

## Copying (pages 265–268)

*Not in HouseCraft yet:* Sticky Mode. To mirror a cabinet, select it, choose **Edit › Reflect About Object** and
Ctrl+click the wall or window to mirror across. **Cabinet Tools › Auto Fillers** puts fillers at the ends of a run.

## Island and breakfast bar (pages 268–274)

1. Open the middle cabinet: width `30"`, and turn it to face left (drag the rotate handle, or set **Facing** to 180°).
2. Put an 18" base either side of it.
3. Select all three and open them: backsplash `0"`. *Not in HouseCraft yet:* a different overhang at the back
   (Uniform off).
4. **Breakfast bar:** a base cabinet behind the island, **Height** `42"`, **Depth** `7"`, as long as the island.
